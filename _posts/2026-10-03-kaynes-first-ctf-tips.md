We asked Kayne for advice for people doing their first CTF. Here's what he said, lightly organized by us.

## Before

- Set up a Linux VM. It doesn't have to be fancy.
- Learn `strings`, `file`, `grep`, and `base64`. You'd be shocked how far they go.
- Find a team. Solving alone is fine, solving with friends is better.

## During

- Read the challenge description twice. The hint is usually in there.
- Take notes as you go. Future you will need them for the writeup.
- If you're stuck for 30+ minutes, move on and come back.
- Ask your team. Nobody is keeping score of who asks the most questions.

## After

- Read writeups for the ones you didn't solve. That's where the real learning happens.
- Write your own writeups. Even short ones.

> "The flag is nice. What you learn on the way to it is the actual prize."
> **Kayne**, being Kayne
